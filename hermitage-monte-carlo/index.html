Elegance, Serenity & Gustave Eiffel’s Glass Masterpiece

"A palace with a soul, where Belle Époque intimacy meets contemporary Riviera grandeur."

Conceived at the pinnacle of Monaco's golden age, Hôtel Hermitage Monte-Carlo is an architectural jewel nestled in the prestigious Carré d'Or. Its breathtaking Winter Garden (Jardin d'Hiver) is crowned with a monumental glass cupola designed by Gustave Eiffel, filtering the radiant Mediterranean sunshine into a cathedral of crystal light.
