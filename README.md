# RP Group of Hotels — Luxury Hotel Management Chain (Patna Region)

A high-converting, animated, luxury hospitality website proposal created for **RP Group of Hotels**, tailored specifically for the **Patna & Bihar regional market** and engineered to drive maximum conversions from **Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram)**, direct bookings, corporate tie-ups, and high-ticket grand wedding banquets.

---

## 🌟 Executive Pitch Summary for the Client

When presenting this proposal to your client (the hotel chain owner in Patna), emphasize these **5 key competitive advantages**:

1. **Engineered for Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram Campaigns)**:
   - Every page features **1-click WhatsApp buttons** pre-filled with inquiry messages.
   - **Sticky Mobile Bottom Bar** (Call, WhatsApp, Instant Booking) ensuring mobile traffic from Instagram and Facebook ads converts with zero friction.
   - Dedicated **Lead Magnet Form** ("Get 2026-2027 Banquet Brochure & Custom Quotation within 15 mins") to capture qualified wedding and event leads directly into the sales CRM.

2. **The Bihar High-Ticket Market: Grand Weddings & Banquets**:
   - Wedding banqueting is the #1 revenue generator for hotels in Bihar (₹5 Lakh to ₹30+ Lakh per event).
   - Features an **Interactive Banquet & Wedding Cost Estimator**: guests can select their event type (*Wedding, Sangeet, Tilak, Corporate Conference*), adjust the guest slider (*100 to 1,800 guests*), pick catering spreads, and receive an instant estimate + direct WhatsApp proposal link.
   - Dedicated showcase for **The Kohinoor Lawn (1,500+ guests)**, **The Royal Crystal Ballroom (600+ guests)**, and live royal catering spreads.

3. **Multi-Property Chain Portfolio Architecture**:
   - Seamlessly positions the chain across 4 key regional locations:
     - **RP Grand Regalia & Convention Centre** (*Bailey Road, Patna*) — 5-Star luxury, destination weddings, swimming pool, presidential suites.
     - **RP Palace & Executive Suites** (*Exhibition Road, Patna*) — High-margin corporate business stays near Patna Junction & Gandhi Maidan.
     - **RP Heritage Resort & Wellness Spa** (*Rajgir Hills*) — Eco-luxury weekend staycations & destination weddings.
     - **RP Bodh Gaya Nirvana Retreat** (*Bodh Gaya*) — Spiritual tourism & pilgrimage stays near Mahabodhi UNESCO temple.

4. **Royal Bihari & Awadhi Culinary Heritage**:
   - Highlighting *The Royal Patliputra* (authentic Champaran Ahuna Handi Mutton, Litti Chokha with smoked desi ghee, Awadhi Dum Biryani).
   - *Saffron Spice* (100% Pure Vegetarian Restaurant with separate kitchens).
   - *Skyline 9* (Patna's premier open-air rooftop lounge).

5. **Direct Booking Incentive System**:
   - Built-in promo coupon code `RPDIRECT` giving guests **Flat 25% Off + Complimentary Buffet Breakfast**, incentivizing travelers to book directly rather than paying 18–25% commission to OTAs (MakeMyTrip, Goibibo, Agoda).
